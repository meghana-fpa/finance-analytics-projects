# Business Insights — FinTech Loan Analytics# Business Insights — FinTech Loan Analytics

## Executive Insights

- The dashboard analyzes approximately 10K loan applications, with around 3.7K approvals and 3.2K disbursements.
- The overall approval rate is approximately 36.85%.
- Personal loans contribute the highest application volume and the largest share of total disbursed amount.
- Digital and DSA channels generate the highest application volumes and approvals compared with other channels.
- Salaried customers represent the largest employment segment in the loan portfolio.
- Low credit score is the leading rejection reason, followed by documentation issues and insufficient income.
- Disbursement performance varies across sales executives, indicating opportunities to identify and replicate high-performing practices.
- Monthly application, approval, and disbursement trends show fluctuations throughout the year that can support operational planning.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the major causes of rejection, particularly low credit scores and documentation issues.
2. Focus on improving conversion from application to approval and from approval to disbursement.
3. Analyze high-performing sales executives to identify effective sales and conversion practices.
4. Strengthen digital and DSA channels because they contribute significantly to application volumes.
5. Develop targeted strategies for customer segments with lower approval or disbursement performance.
6. Use monthly performance trends for better sales planning and resource allocation.

## Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard provides a consolidated view of the loan funnel, channel performance, loan types, employment segments, rejection reasons, and sales executive performance. The analysis can support data-driven decisions related to loan acquisition, approval efficiency, sales productivity, and disbursement performance.
