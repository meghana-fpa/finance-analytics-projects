# Business Insights — FinTech Loan Analytics

## 📊 Executive Insights

- The dashboard analyzes approximately **10K loan applications**, with around **3.7K approvals** and **3.2K disbursements**.
- The overall approval rate is approximately **36.85%**.
- Personal loans contribute the highest application volume and the largest share of total disbursed amount.
- Digital and DSA channels generate significant application and approval volumes.
- Salaried customers represent the largest employment segment in the loan portfolio.
- Low credit score is the leading rejection reason, followed by documentation issues and insufficient income.
- Disbursement performance varies across sales executives, providing an opportunity to identify high-performing practices.
- Monthly application, approval, and disbursement trends fluctuate throughout the year and can support operational planning.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Investigate major rejection drivers, particularly low credit scores and documentation issues.
2. Focus on improving conversion from application to approval and from approval to disbursement.
3. Analyze high-performing sales executives to identify effective sales and conversion practices.
4. Strengthen digital and DSA channels while monitoring the quality and conversion of leads generated through these channels.
5. Develop targeted strategies for customer segments with lower approval or disbursement performance.
6. Use monthly performance trends to support sales planning and resource allocation.

## 🎯 Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard provides a consolidated view of the loan funnel, channel performance, loan types, employment segments, rejection reasons, and sales executive performance.

The analysis can support data-driven decisions related to **loan acquisition, approval efficiency, sales productivity, and disbursement performance**.
