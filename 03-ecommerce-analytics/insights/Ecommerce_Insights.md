# E-commerce Customer & Revenue Analytics — Business Insights

## 📊 Project Summary

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand revenue performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and business trends.

The Power BI dashboard was designed to convert transactional data into actionable business insights for monitoring sales performance and identifying growth opportunities.

---

## 🔎 Key Business Insights

### 1. Revenue Performance

- Revenue performance was analyzed across time periods, customer segments, and product categories.
- Trend analysis helps identify periods of stronger and weaker sales performance.
- Monitoring revenue trends can help businesses understand demand patterns and plan sales activities accordingly.

### 2. Customer Analysis

- Customer-level analysis was used to understand purchasing behavior and identify high-value customer segments.
- Customer segmentation helps distinguish different purchasing patterns and revenue contributions.
- Understanding customer behavior can support targeted marketing and retention strategies.

### 3. Product Performance

- Product-level analysis was performed to identify products contributing significantly to orders and revenue.
- Comparing product performance helps identify strong-performing products as well as products requiring further attention.
- Product performance can be monitored to support inventory planning, promotions, and assortment decisions.

### 4. Sales & Order Trends

- Order volume was analyzed to understand overall sales activity and changes over time.
- Comparing order trends with revenue provides a broader view of business performance.
- Periods with changes in order activity can be investigated further to understand underlying customer or product drivers.

### 5. Business Segmentation

- The dashboard enables performance analysis across relevant business dimensions such as customers, products, and categories.
- Segmentation makes it easier to identify specific areas contributing to overall business performance.
- This supports more focused business analysis instead of relying only on overall KPIs.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### Customer Strategy
Focus on understanding high-value and frequently purchasing customers and develop targeted retention initiatives.

### Product Strategy
Monitor high-performing products closely while investigating products with relatively weaker order or revenue contribution.

### Revenue Monitoring
Track revenue and order trends regularly to identify changes in sales performance and investigate the factors driving those changes.

### Data-Driven Decision Making
Use customer, product, and revenue segmentation together rather than evaluating individual KPIs in isolation.
